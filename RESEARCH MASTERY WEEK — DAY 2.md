**Type 01 — Survey / Questionnaire Data**

Suitable Research Areas:
▸ Psychology and behavior studies
▸ Education research
▸ Social science
▸ User experience studies
▸ Healthcare perception studies

කොහොමද collect කරන්නේ:

Google Forms හෝ KoboToolbox use කරලා survey design කරන්න. University student communities, social media groups, professional networks ඇතුළ distribute කරන්න.

Minimum sample size: Research type අනුව varies. General rule — n=100 minimum. Statistical power analysis කරලා exact number determine කරන්න.

ලංකාවේ advantage: University network ඇතිව students 200-500 reach කිරීම days ඇතුළ possible.

Tools:
🔧 Google Forms — Free, easy, automatic sheets export
🔧 KoboToolbox — Research grade, offline collection possible
🔧 Typeform — Better UX, higher completion rate
🔧 SurveyCTO — Field research grade, advanced logic

**Data Collection ගේදී Ethical Rules — Skip කරන්නේ නෑ:**

Ethics — Research ගේ foundation. Violation — Research career destroy කරනවා.

**Rule 01 — Informed Consent:**
Human subjects involve ගේදී — participants ලා clearly inform කරන්නට ඕනෙ.

✅ Research purpose
✅ Data use කරන ආකාරය
✅ Anonymity guarantee
✅ Withdrawal right
✅ Contact information

Written consent form — mandatory.

**Rule 02 — Anonymization:**
Personal identifying information — dataset ඇතුළ නෑ.

Names → ID codes
Phone numbers → Remove
Addresses → District level only
Photos with faces → Blur හෝ consent

**Rule 03 — Institutional Ethics Approval:**
Human subjects involve ගේදී — University Ethics Committee approval ගන්නට ඕනෙ.

Process time: 2-4 weeks. Early apply කරන්න.

**Rule 04 — Data Security:**
Collected data — secure storage.

▸ Password protected folders
▸ Google Drive with restricted access
▸ No sharing with unauthorized persons
▸ Clear data retention policy

**Rule 05 — Transparency:**
Methodology paper ඇතුළ clearly document කරන්නට ඕනෙ.

▸ Collection method
▸ Sample size justification
▸ Inclusion/exclusion criteria
▸ Limitations honestly state

---

**Dataset Quality Control — Collect කළාට පසු:**

Data collect කළා. දැන් quality check.

**Step 01 — Completeness Check:**
Missing values ගණන count කරන්න. 20%+ missing — re-collection consider කරන්න.

```python
import pandas as pd
df = pd.read_csv('your_dataset.csv')
print(df.isnull().sum())
print(df.isnull().mean() * 100)
```

**Step 02 — Class Balance Check:**
Classification research ගේදී — classes balanced ද?

Imbalanced dataset → Biased model → Wrong conclusions

```python
print(df['target_column'].value_counts())
print(df['target_column'].value_counts(normalize=True))
```

**Step 03 — Outlier Detection:**
Extreme values — data entry errors ද, genuine outliers ද?

```python
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt
df.boxplot()
plt.show()
```

**Step 04 — Duplicate Check:**
Same record twice enter වෙලා තියෙනවා ද?

```python
print(df.duplicated().sum())
df = df.drop_duplicates()
```

**Step 05 — Inter-rater Reliability (Annotation ගේදී):**
Multiple annotators use කළොත් — agreement measure කරන්න.

Cohen's Kappa > 0.7 — acceptable agreement.
